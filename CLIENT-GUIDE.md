# How to update your website

**Your website:** https://ember-salt-astro.akarshbandi82.workers.dev
**Your editor:** https://ember-salt.sanity.studio

Everything you can change on this site is in one place. You never need to
contact anyone to change a word, a price, or a photo.

---

## 1. Open the editor

Go to **https://ember-salt.sanity.studio** and sign in with the email address
we invited. If it asks for a one-time code, check your inbox.

*Forgot the login? Ask us — it takes two minutes to reset.*

---

## 2. Choose what to change

The left-hand column is your whole website:

| In the left column | What it controls |
|---|---|
| **Pages** | Home, Menu, Our Story, Reservations |
| **Journal entries** | The written posts / news items |
| **Site settings** | The logo text, menu links, and the footer |

Click any page name to open it.

---

## 3. Edit next to your live site

When you open a page you get two panels:

- **Left panel — your website.** This is the real page, so you can see what you
  are changing while you change it.
- **Right panel — the fields.** Each box is one thing you can edit: a heading,
  a paragraph, a photo, a button.

Click any text in the left panel and the matching box on the right highlights,
so you always know what you are about to change.

---

## 4. The three things you will do most

**Change a heading or a paragraph**
Click the field on the right, select the words, type your new ones. The left
panel updates as you type.

**Swap a photo**
Click the photo field, then choose an existing image or upload a new one. A
photo should be a **.jpg** or **.webp** file, ideally under 5 MB and wider than
1200 pixels.

**Change a button** (for example "Book a table" → "Reserve now")
There are two boxes for every button: one for the **words** and one for the
**link**. Set the words here, and the link to a page on your own site, starting
with a `/` — for example `/visit`.

---

## 5. Publish

Nothing goes live until you press **Publish** (the button at the top right).

- You can make as many changes as you like first — they stay as a draft.
- After you press **Publish**, your website updates in **about one minute**.
- Press **Refresh** in the left panel to see your published change.

---

## 6. If you make a mistake

**Before you publish:** press **Discard changes** at the top right. Everything
goes back to what visitors currently see.

**After you publish:** open the page in the editor and press **History** in the
top right. You can see previous versions and restore one. Versions are kept for
a few days, so if something looks wrong, tell us within that time and we will
put it right.

**If it looks broken and you cannot fix it:** press **Discard changes** if you
have not published, or call us. There is no harm done by pressing Publish on
something wrong — it can always be put back.

---

## 7. Two things worth knowing

**Do not change "Web address"** unless we ask you to. It is the web link for
that page. Changing it breaks the address your customers have bookmarked, and
the old link stops working.

**"Site settings" affects the whole site.** That is where the logo text, the
menu links and the footer live. A change there appears on every page at once.

---

## 8. What we handle for you

You do not need to ask us for any of these — but if you want to, we can:

- New pages, or a change to the design or layout
- Photographs resized or compressed for faster loading
- The seasonal menu, or a banner for a special event
- Anything that needs a code change behind the scenes

Those are part of your care plan. Content changes — words, prices, photos —
are yours to make, any time, at no cost.
